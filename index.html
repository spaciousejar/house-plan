A four-sided plot has only five free measurements once a side and an angle are fixed. With four sides plus two diagonals you have six, so one must give. This plan holds East 32′, South 28′, West 32′, North 41.5′ and the 51.7′ diagonal exact. The 44.1′ diagonal then works out at 41.1′, which is 3.0 ft shorter than the tape. Either way the area lands between 1,056 and 1,080 sq ft, so 1,056 sq ft is the safe figure to quote. A site re-survey will settle which measurement is out.

Ground floor · living dining, kitchen, bedroom, workroom

Entry at the road wall into the living dining room, which runs the full width of the front band. The kitchen and utility are on the left. A 4′-8″ cased opening, not a door, leads into the hall, so the living room and the hall read as one space from the front door to the stair. The hall runs west to the bath and store, and the bedroom sits on the south wall with a door to the rear verandah. Numbers in the area column are measured from the drawn outline, so they differ slightly from width × depth wherever a slanted outer wall clips the room.

First floor · library, study, master bedroom, sky terrace

The lounge sits over the living room, the library and dressing over the kitchen and utility, and the master bedroom over the ground bedroom, so every wall and drain line stacks. The lounge opens to the hall through the same 4′-8″ cased opening as the living room below it. The stair arrives at the same spot on both plans. The dashed rectangle in the lounge is the floor kept clear of furniture and of every door swing, 6′ × 7′-10″. The terrace is the tapered rear of the plot, open to sky, with the 4′ × 4′ pad set 2.0 ft clear of the parapet on every side. It also carries the terrace garden and the services: planters along the west edge and along the stair side, a solar array on stands near the stair head, and the water tank plinth behind the stair head.
