A four-sided plot has only five free measurements once a side and an angle are fixed. With four sides plus two diagonals you have six, so one must give. This plan holds East 32′, South 28′, West 32′, North 41.5′ and the 51.7′ diagonal exact. The 44.1′ diagonal then works out at 41.1′, which is 3.0 ft shorter than the tape. Either way the area lands between 1,056 and 1,080 sq ft, so 1,056 sq ft is the safe figure to quote. A site re-survey will settle which measurement is out.
