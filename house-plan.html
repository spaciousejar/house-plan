North is up, so the road and the main entrance are on the right (East) side. Dashed red lines are the given diagonals (labelled 44.1′ / 48.11′ as measured). Ground floor · 1 Bedroom, Living, Kitchen, Bath

Entry: porch (2 ft deep) → living/dining. Stair hall sits on the road side and serves the first floor from the same stacked position. Bath and store are 6′ × 6′; the bedroom opens off the hall.

First floor · Bedroom + Study room, Lounge, Bath

Lounge sits over the living/dining, study room over the kitchen, master bedroom over the ground-floor bedroom, and the open terrace over the rear utility. The stair is directly above the ground-floor stair.
